Safety Injury & Damage (SID) Report

Use this form to report incidents involving a potential safety concern, personal injury, or property damage related to an Ideal Living product. Please provide complete and accurate information and upload all available supporting evidence.

⚠️ Stop use immediately if the unit sparks internally or the power cord is damaged — instruct the customer right away.
📦 If the incident is classified Critical or High, request collection of the product for inspection.

Restricted to QA/internal use once this form is migrated into Zendesk. For this prototype, the agent selects severity — QA reviews and confirms it afterward.

Severity guidance
Critical — death, hospitalization, serious injury, fire spread, explosion, electrocution, or severe/significant property damage.
High — injury requiring medical attention, significant property damage, major water damage, or a serious safety event without confirmed critical consequences.
Moderate — minor injury, contained property damage, or a safety concern that does not involve medical treatment or significant damage.
